## Recommended validation workflow
1. Open each target in logged-in Chrome.
2. Confirm: audience fit, group rules, recent activity, promo tolerance, and whether comments or DMs are better than posts.
3. Mark each as one of:
   - high-fit outreach target
   - observe only
   - not worth using
4. Keep a note of the post angle most likely to land:
   - fundraiser pain removal
   - organizer simplicity
   - cleaner live board sharing
   - office/community game-day experience
