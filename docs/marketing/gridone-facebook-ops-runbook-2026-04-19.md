## Operating guardrails
- No ad spend, boosts, or paid actions without explicit approval.
- No offensive, deceptive, or spammy posting.
- No rapid-fire bursts that look automated.
- Do not respond instantly just because a comment appears.
- Prefer useful participation over hard-sell behavior.
- Avoid repetitive copy-paste across groups.
- Respect each group's rules before posting.

## Human-like pacing rules
- Prefer 1 primary page post at a time, not multiple back-to-back posts.
- Space comments/replies naturally instead of immediate reaction.
- Stagger any group outreach instead of same-minute posting across targets.
- Use the page for clean, practical content first, then measured outreach.
